# Evidence Register: The Motivation Architecture

*The research cited in "The Motivation Architecture", and where the paper cites it.*

---

## How to read this register

Each work below is cited in the paper. The second column gives the sections that cite it.

Each work has one entry in Runapp's evidence registry, with one exception: Kant's *Groundwork*, cited for a principle, is a work of philosophy and not a study. The entry records what the study itself shows and how much confidence the specification places in it. When a later reading of a study shows that an entry said more than the study did, the entry is corrected and the rules resting on it are read again.

The register names the studies. It does not say which value of the specification a study sets. That is in the specification, which is shared under NDA. Bibliographic details are checked against the publisher.
